Verified on a migrated repo: `Store.commit_project_changes` runs `git add .project` from the repo root and fails with "paths are ignored by one of your .gitignore files"; `push_project_changes` resolves HEAD at the root and pushes main. Fix: stage and commit with cwd inside the store (`git -C .project add -A .` etc.) so the commit lands on whichever branch owns .project, and push the branch that `store_git_state` reports. Plain-directory behaviour and the auto-generated message must stay byte-identical (pinned by test_commit_scope / test_auto_commit_messages).
